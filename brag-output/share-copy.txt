Never miss an opening at Apple. 

Apple roles often fill up within hours of going live. Refreshing jobs.apple.com manually is a losing battle.

I built Jobs Monitor — a free, lightweight native macOS menu bar app that tracks jobs.apple.com & careers.apple.com in real-time.

⚡ Dual-channel: Public + AppleConnect SSO Internal mode
🌍 24 global tech hubs (Cupertino, Austin, London, Bengaluru, Singapore, Munich...)
🔔 Instant native macOS alerts the second new roles go live
🔒 100% on-device & private. Zero telemetry. No accounts required.

One-line terminal install:
/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arunofhyd/JobsMonitor/main/install-jobsmonitor.command)"

Open source: https://github.com/arunofhyd/JobsMonitor

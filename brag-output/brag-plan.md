# Jobs Monitor Launch Plan — Apple Careers Focus

## Goal
Showcase Jobs Monitor as the essential, native macOS menu bar app for tracking **jobs.apple.com** and internal **careers.apple.com** in real-time.

## Positioning
- **Audience**: Engineers, designers, engineering managers, and Apple employees tracking public openings or internal transfers.
- **Hook**: Apple openings fill up in hours. Manual refreshing is too slow.
- **Solution**: Jobs Monitor lives quietly in your macOS menu bar with instant system alerts.
- **Key Features**:
  - Dual-Channel: Public + AppleConnect SSO Internal mode
  - 24 Global Tech Hubs (Cupertino, Austin, London, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Singapore, Munich)
  - 100% On-device, zero telemetry, no accounts needed
  - Instant local search dashboard
- **Format**: 20-second 1080p native video with H.264/AAC encoding.

Why use Jobs Monitor?

🌐

Public Tracking (Open to All)

Anyone can track new openings directly on jobs.apple.com across 20+ global countries and 24 major tech hubs with zero login required.

🔐

 Apple Employee Internal Mode

Specially for current Apple employees: Integrated AppleConnect SSO authentication via a secure embedded sheet to unlock and monitor internal-only requisitions on careers.apple.com.

📊

Dual-Channel 40+40 Quotas

Independent 40-role aggregation pipelines ensure internal and public listings never compete for space. Browse Both, Internal Only, or Public Only.

🏷️

Smart Seniority Detection

Automatically classifies positions into Intern, Early (1–3 yrs), Mid (4–7 yrs), Advanced (7+ yrs), and Director levels based on job requirements.

🌍

Global Tech Hubs

Track openings across 20+ countries and 24 major tech hubs (Cupertino, Austin, London, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Singapore, Munich, etc.).

🔔

Instant System Alerts

Get instant, non-intrusive macOS system alerts whenever new matching roles are posted in your tracked regions.

⏱️

Custom Checks & Digest

Set background check intervals from 5 minutes to 6 hours, plus scheduled weekday morning digests at 10:00 AM.

🔒

100% On-Device

Your search queries and auth tokens never leave your Mac. Connects directly to Apple portals with zero intermediate servers.

🚀

Native Swift & Menu Bar UI

Adaptive monochrome icon for Light & Dark modes. Built natively in Swift with SMAppService login launch, zero dependencies, and no Dock clutter.

How it works

1

Select your hubs & auth mode

Choose your preferred countries or cities to track public openings. Current Apple employees can toggle Internal Mode for direct AppleConnect SSO authentication to view internal-only roles.

2

Silent background tracking

Jobs Monitor checks quietly in the background at your chosen frequency and maintains your 40+40 role cache across internal and public pipelines.

3

Open the dashboard

Click the menu bar icon to view active positions, filter by experience level, and jump directly into jobs.apple.com (public) or careers.apple.com (internal) with one click.

Installation

⚡ One-Click Terminal Install (Recommended)

The easiest and fastest way to install Jobs Monitor. Simply copy and paste this command into your Terminal:

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arunofhyd/JobsMonitor/main/install-jobsmonitor.command)"

Manual Installation (Fallback)

  1. Download install-jobsmonitor.command file manually.
  2. Open Terminal (⌘+Space, type "Terminal", press Enter).
  3. Type sh — that's s, h, then a space.
  4. Drag and drop the downloaded file into the Terminal window.
  5. Press Enter and follow the on-screen prompts, then drag Jobs Monitor onto Applications.
user@macbook ~ % /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arunofhyd/JobsMonitor/main/install-jobsmonitor.command)"
==> Compiling and launching Jobs Monitor…

First time only: the installer builds the application natively on your Mac so macOS Gatekeeper trusts it completely with no security warnings.
